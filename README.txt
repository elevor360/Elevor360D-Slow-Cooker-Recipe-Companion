Elevor360D Slow Cooker Recipe Companion V1

50 individually drafted slow cooker recipes. Large graphic timers, favourites, shopping lists, weekly meal planner, personal recipes, backup and restore, offline PWA, rotating adverts.

Upload all website files and advertising folder to your GitHub Pages repository root. Keep Elevor360D_Slow_Cooker_Recipe_Manager_V1.html on your own computer; do not upload it publicly. Open manager, import recipes.json, edit and export updated recipes.json. The service worker fetches updated recipes.json when online and uses its cached copy offline.

Safety: Times are estimates, not guarantees. Do not cook frozen meat; use a thermometer for safe doneness. Do not use uncooked dried kidney beans in a slow cooker. Recipes have not been kitchen-tested individually.
